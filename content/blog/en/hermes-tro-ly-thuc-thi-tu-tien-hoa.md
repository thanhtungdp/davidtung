Most companies use AI as a **Q&A tool**. Answers get longer and prettier — but humans still do the work.

## From chatbot to execution assistant

Hermes is built around one idea: every time it completes a task correctly, the agent **packages the method as a skill**. Next time, it doesn't need to be taught again.

## Three departments, one orchestration core

- **Sales**: quotes, emails, CRM sync.
- **Marketing**: on-brand content, visuals, short videos.
- **Delivery**: briefings, weekly reports, reminders.

## Measured by outcomes

A good agent isn't measured by the number of answers, but by **the number of tasks finished** without a human stepping in.
