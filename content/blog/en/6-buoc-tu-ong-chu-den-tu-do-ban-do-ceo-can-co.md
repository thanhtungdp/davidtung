Good CEOs get stuck not for lack of ability, but because they **work in the wrong order**. These six steps are a required sequence, not a menu.

## From the foundation

1. **Mastery** — own your cash flow, time, and core numbers.
2. **Niche** — choose the customers you serve best.
3. **Leverage** — systematize so work runs without you.

## To freedom

4. **Team** — owners, metrics, and a meeting rhythm.
5. **Synergy** — departments reinforce rather than pull against each other.
6. **Freedom** — the business grows without depending on the founder.

## Where does AI fit on the map?

AI is the strongest lever at the **Leverage** step. But if you skip Mastery and Niche, AI just helps you do the wrong thing faster.
